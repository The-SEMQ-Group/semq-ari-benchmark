# Retired claims index

This index records where retired or softened claims still appeared in decks, notes and secondary
prose on 2026-09-16, and what was done at each location. The authoritative sources are the
[repository audit](REPOSITORY_AUDIT.md) and the [retraction ledger](retractions.md). The paper
draft (`docs/paper/latex/ari.tex`) was not edited; its number check is in
[CLAIM_TO_EVIDENCE](paper/CLAIM_TO_EVIDENCE.md).

"Fixed" means the text was replaced because the correct statement is unambiguous from the audit.
"Marked" means the historical text was kept and the page carries one line at the top that names the
claim ids and links to this index.
